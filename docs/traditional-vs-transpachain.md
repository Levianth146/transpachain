# Traditional Charity vs TranspaChain

Key differences donors and organizations should know — useful for demo Q&A and stakeholder conversations.

| Aspect | Traditional | TranspaChain |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **Fund custody** | Org holds funds directly | Locked in on-chain escrow vault |
| **Release trigger** | Internal approval | DAO vote after milestone proof |
| **Transparency** | Periodic reports | Every tx & vote on Sepolia explorer |
| **Failed campaign** | Refund policy varies | Automatic refund eligibility on-chain |
| **Donor proof** | Receipt email | Impact NFT + tx hash |
